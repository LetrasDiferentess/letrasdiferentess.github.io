Do Fancy Fonts Work Everywhere?

No. The appearance depends on an app's character restrictions, the operating system and its available glyphs. Missing glyphs can appear as boxes. Try a simpler style if necessary. Avoid using decorated Unicode for passwords, account identifiers or important instructions. Screen readers may pronounce some styled characters differently from ordinary letters.

How do I copy and paste fancy text?

Type your text, choose a result and press Copy. Paste it into the destination application and verify its appearance.

Do I need to install fonts?

No font installation is needed for Unicode copy-and-paste text. Separate font resources may be used for handwriting image exports.

Why are some letters displayed as squares?

The receiving app or device may not have a font glyph for that Unicode symbol. Use another style or standard characters.
