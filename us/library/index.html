About this collection

These twelve subject areas define the editorial architecture for 96 planned technical documents. The articles are not yet published, so this directory describes their intended coverage rather than presenting unfinished pages as available references. As documents are reviewed and released, they will be connected here through meaningful relationships, not duplicated category URLs.

Standards-led

Research grounded in primary technical sources such as Unicode specifications, W3C guidance, ICU and official implementation documentation.

Reproducible

Where applicable, examples should make behavior and edge cases testable rather than merely describing them.

Useful beyond this website

Designed to support clear technical discussions and honest citations when a community's policies permit them.
